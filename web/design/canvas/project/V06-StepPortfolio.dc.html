Design notes

Changed: Wizard shell: new 9-step stepper (labels now match the step count; 'Submitted' is its own screen, not a ninth dot), one form card, Back and Save and continue in the card footer, tips or live preview in the aside. Drop zone, progress toward the 5 photo minimum, then a grid with Cover marker and remove buttons.
