Your cancellation policy

If a client cancels a paid booking, this decides how much of your price they get back. Until you pick, clients see Flexible.

Two rules apply to every policy

The Eventtz service fee is always refunded. A client who cancels within 24 hours of paying, with the event at least 7 days away, gets a full refund.

Choosing a policy
  • Flexible wins more small bookings. Strict protects dates you turn other work away for.
  • Clients see the policy on your profile and again before they pay.
  • Most vendors pick Moderate.
You can change this later in Settings. A booking keeps the policy it was made under.
Design notes

Changed: Cancellation policy is now its own step (step 5 of 9), straight after Pricing, instead of a section inside Additional info. The three presets sit side by side with their refund tiers visible, so a vendor compares them before choosing.

Other states: Loading: skeleton option cards. Error: none possible, a preset is always selected (Flexible by default). Empty: Flexible preselected with the help text saying so.
