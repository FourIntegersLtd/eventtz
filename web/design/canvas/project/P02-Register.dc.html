Design notes

Changed: The route from today's register page (You are here, numbered stops, destination flag) moves into the purple panel, replacing the marketing line. Client or vendor is a segmented control at the top, so the choice is clear before any field. Referral banner sits under the title.

Other states: Vendor selected: title becomes Create your vendor account and the route switches to Your route as a vendor (Set up, Get requests, Agree optional, destination Get paid: accept, the client pays, payout after the event). Mobile: the panel collapses to the compact strip with See how it works. Inline field errors under each input. Loading: button says Creating account.

Components: ez-auth split, ez-seg, ez-banner--brand, ez-check, ez-field
