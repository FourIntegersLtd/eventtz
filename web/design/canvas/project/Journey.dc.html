One booking, three points of view

A booking has two lifecycles: the request (what was agreed) and the payment (where the money is). Every screen below shows both with the same badges, so a client, a vendor and an admin always describe the same booking the same way.

Held funds

Checkout always pays Eventtz. The vendor is paid by an explicit transfer after completion. That is what lets a dispute stop a payout.

Tiered cancels

A client cancel splits the money by the vendor's preset. The vendor's share is held until their payouts are set up (V22).

Fixes in this redesign

Quotes get a visible card for the client (C12). Payments get one home per role (C17, V25). New vendors land on their profile after sign up.
