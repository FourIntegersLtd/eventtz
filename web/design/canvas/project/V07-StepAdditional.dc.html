Design notes

Changed: Wizard shell: new 9-step stepper (labels now match the step count; 'Submitted' is its own screen, not a ninth dot), one form card, Back and Save and continue in the card footer, tips or live preview in the aside. Cancellation policy is now its own step (step 5), so this step is three optional groups (online, dietary, documents) with upload status as a badge per document.

Other states: Loading: skeleton fields. Error: inline message under the field plus a banner above the card. Empty: not applicable, every step opens with saved answers or sensible defaults. Upload error: row shows danger text and a Retry button. Empty: all optional, so Save and continue is always available.

Components: ez-option, ez-table, ez-banner, ez-list-row, ez-check, ez-icon-tile, ez-badge, ez-field
