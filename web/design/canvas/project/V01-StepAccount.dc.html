Design notes

Changed: Wizard shell: new 9-step stepper (labels now match the step count; 'Submitted' is its own screen, not a ninth dot), one form card, Back and Save and continue in the card footer, tips or live preview in the aside. The old 9-label header showed Submitted as a step; it is now a separate screen (V09).

Other states: Loading: skeleton fields. Error: inline message under the field plus a banner above the card. Empty: not applicable, every step opens with saved answers or sensible defaults. Phone with a bad format shows the error text and keeps the button enabled.

Components: ez-stepper, ez-field, ez-input, ez-card, ez-badge, ez-progress, ez-callout
