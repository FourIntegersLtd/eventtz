4. Payments and held funds

When you accept a quote you pay Eventtz through Stripe Checkout. Eventtz holds the payment and pays the vendor after your event has taken place.

Prices shown are set by the vendor. You are told the full amount, including any fees, before you pay.

What changed in this version

Cancellation terms are now agreed at the point of payment and shown on your booking.

5. Cancellations and refunds

If you cancel, the refund depends on the vendor's cancellation terms, which are saved with your booking when you pay. Refunds go back to the original card.

6. Disputes

If something goes wrong, raise a dispute from your booking. Payouts to the vendor are paused while Eventtz staff review it.
