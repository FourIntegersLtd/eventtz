How to budget for a 100 guest wedding

EAEventtz team · Fri 9 Oct 2026 · 8 min read

A hundred guests is a common size and an easy one to overspend on. Start with a total, then split it before you speak to any vendor.

Start with the big three

Venue and catering usually take half of the budget. For 100 guests, expect catering from about £35.00 to £60.00 per head, so £3,500.00 to £6,000.00 before drinks.

Tip: ask each caterer what a quote includes, such as staff, crockery and service charge, so you compare like with like.

Then music, flowers and photography

A DJ for an evening usually costs £500.00 to £900.00. Photography varies most, so look at full galleries rather than highlights.
