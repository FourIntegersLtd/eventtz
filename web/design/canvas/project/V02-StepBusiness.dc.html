Design notes

Changed: Wizard shell: new 9-step stepper (labels now match the step count; 'Submitted' is its own screen, not a ninth dot), one form card, Back and Save and continue in the card footer, tips or live preview in the aside. Category and event types are chips instead of long radio lists; the preview card updates as you type.

Other states: Loading: skeleton fields. Error: inline message under the field plus a banner above the card. Empty: not applicable, every step opens with saved answers or sensible defaults. Category 'Other' swaps the form for a 'Category not available yet' card with the waitlist link. Duplicate business name shows an inline error.

Components: ez-chip, ez-field, ez-vcard, ez-stepper, ez-card
