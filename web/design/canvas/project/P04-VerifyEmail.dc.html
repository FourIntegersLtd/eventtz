Design notes

Changed: The two happy-path states are drawn together: spinner first, then confirmation with a single Sign in button. The expired-link banner is shown beneath as the failure state.

Panel art: A light collage in the spirit of contemporary African art fills the empty brand panel: mudcloth marks, kente bands, Ndebele zigzags, dot grids and triangle borders. The shapes are the product: an arch for venues, a sun of rings for music and celebration, a rising half sun for the day itself, woven tiles for the people who make it, and a dotted route joining them. Decorative only, hidden on mobile. Register keeps its route steps instead. The earlier floral option is kept on the Auth panel art board.

Other states: Expired link: danger banner plus email field and Request a new link. Already verified: same as verified. Network error: retry button.

Components: ez-auth split, ez-spinner, ez-icon-tile--success, ez-banner--danger
