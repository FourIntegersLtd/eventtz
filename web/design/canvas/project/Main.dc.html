Design language v1 · October 2026

Celebration, made calm.

The event is the exciting part. Everything around it, finding a vendor, agreeing a price, paying and getting paid, should feel quiet, clear and trustworthy. Purple carries the brand, gold marks the moments worth celebrating, and everything else steps back.

Client Vendor Admin
Principles
Soft by default

As on the live browse page: pale lavender page, white cards on a hairline, near-black text at 400 to 500 weight, pale pills. Purple is for the one main action and small accents, never large blocks.

One way to do each thing

One button, one card, one field, one badge. A screen is assembled from shared parts, never restyled by hand.

Status is always visible

Every booking shows where it is in its journey and what happens next, with a word and a colour, never colour alone.

Money reads like a receipt

Prices in pounds, tabular figures, the total last and largest. No surprises between quote, checkout and payout.

Same kit, three rooms

Clients, vendors and admins share tokens and components. Only the shell changes: browse-first, work-focused, dense. All three are light.

Shape · three radii
10 control
16 card
pill

Buttons, inputs, nav items: 10. Cards, modals, drawers, banners: 16. Badges, chips, avatars: pill. Nothing else. Today the app uses eight.

Elevation · barely there
1 resting
2 raised
3 overlay

Cards rest on a 1px line, not a shadow. Raised is for hover and featured cards. Overlay is only for modals, drawers and toasts.

Spacing · 4-point

4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64. Card padding 24, page padding 32, section gap 40, form field gap 16.

Motion

One easing: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Two speeds: 150ms for hovers and presses, 220ms for modals, drawers and toasts entering.

Lists stagger in once. Lottie appears only at a start or an ending: welcome, success, empty, failure. Everything respects reduced motion.

Icons

Lucide only, stroke 1.75, 18px in buttons, 20px in nav, 22px in icon tiles. Drop react-icons. Never emoji.

Accessibility

44px minimum tap target, including small buttons in dense tables. 3px Brand 400 focus ring. Every text pair passes AA. Status always pairs a word with its colour.

Voice
Say

"Waiting for Mama Ade's Kitchen to reply. Most vendors answer within a day."

"You have been paid £1,125.00 for the Okafor wedding."

Avoid

"Status: PENDING_VENDOR_RESPONSE"

"Oops! Something went wrong :(" or any em dash in product copy.

Plain UK English. Sentence case for every title and button. Name the person and the event. Buttons say what happens: "Pay £450 deposit", not "Continue".
