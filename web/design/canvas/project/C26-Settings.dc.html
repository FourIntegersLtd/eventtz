Design notes

Changed: Settings is one calm column with three tabs from the brief: Account, Privacy and More. Profile, password and notification preferences sit together, and Delete account is isolated in a danger zone with the blockers stated up front. The app also has a Referrals tab, which can stay under More.

Other states: Saved: toast "Settings saved". Delete: modal "Delete your account?" asks for the current password and lists blockers if any. Wrong password: inline error. Loading: skeleton fields. Error: failure banner.

Components: ez-tabs, ez-card, ez-field, ez-input, switches, ez-badge, ez-btn--danger-quiet
