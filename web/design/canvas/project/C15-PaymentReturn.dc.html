Design notes

Changed: The return from Stripe used to be a toast on top of the booking list. It is now its own page with a clear holding state, then a success state that says what happens to the money and what to do next.

Other states: Cancelled at Stripe: neutral banner "Payment cancelled. Try again when you're ready." with Pay again. Still pending after 60 seconds: warning with Refresh and Contact support. Payment failed: danger banner and the card-declined reason from Stripe.

Components: ez-spinner, ez-empty, ez-card--raised, ez-lines, ez-banner, ez-badge, ez-grid-2
